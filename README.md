# EXCEL---McDonalds-Dashboard
Excel Dashboard from Raw Data for McDanalds

**Overview**
This repository contains the sales analytics dashboard and cleaned dataset for McDonald's regional performance in India. The Excel dashboard (McDonald Clean Data.xlsx) provides interactive insights into sales revenue, profitability, customer demographics, ordering patterns, and item performance across states.

**File Structure**

McDonald Clean Data.xlsx: Excel workbook containing raw order records, cleaned data tables, KPI metrics, pivot tables, and the interactive dashboard layout.
McDonald Dashboard.png: Image preview of the dynamic Excel sales dashboard.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Total Sales: ₹35,52,023.00
Average Sale Price: ₹177.60
Total Profit: ₹13,69,249.00

**Dashboard Features & Visualizations**

1. Interactive Slicers / Filters

State: Chandigarh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand.
Gender: Female, Male, Other.
Category: Beverage, Burger, Dessert, Fries, Sides.
Months (OrderDate): Jan through Dec.

2. Regional Analysis

Sum of Total Price by State (Bar Chart): Compares total sales revenue across states, with Uttar Pradesh leading revenue generation.
Sum of Profit by State (Horizontal Bar Chart): Ranks profit contributions by state, with Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand generating top profits.

3. Sales & Volume Analysis

Monthly Net Price Trend (Bar Chart): Tracks monthly sales revenue throughout the year.
Monthly Profit Trend (Horizontal Bar Chart): Breaks down total profit performance month by month.
Count of Orders by Category (Bar Chart): Visualizes order volume across product lines, led by Burgers (5,012 orders) and Beverages (4,994 orders).

4. Demographic Breakdown

Gender Share (3D Pie Chart):

Male: 49%
Female: 47%
Other: 4%
